Short-term rental alerts — South Lake Tahoe, CA

South Lake Tahoe caps residential Vacation Home Rental permits at 900 and has been reshaping the rules since Measure T. Permit caps, waitlists, and ordinance amendments land here fast — Ordinest watches the city's VHR page daily.

Recent official activity

Effective August 21, 2026

New residential VHR applications moved to a waitlist

As the city approaches the 900-permit residential cap, new VHR permit applications went onto a waitlist. Tourist Core and commercial zones are not affected by the cap or the waitlist.

Effective April 23, 2026

Ordinance 2026-1203 took effect

An amended ordinance changed VHR permitting and operation rules (midnight, April 23, 2026). The city posts what changed directly on the VHR page under "Amended Ordinance Changes."

Standing rules

Permits are required — no exceptions

Operating a VHR without a permit is a violation, and the city runs an enforcement program. Application routing (preferred applicants first, then general) is defined by City Council Resolution 2025-052.
