Short-term rental alerts — Palm Springs, CA

Palm Springs runs one of California's strictest vacation rental programs: certificate caps, annual contract limits, and a neighborhood density cap. Ordinest checks the city's official pages daily so an ordinance change never reaches you as a fine.

Recent official activity

July 8, 2026

Ordinance No. 2133 adopted

City Council amended multiple provisions of Chapter 5.25 of the Municipal Code (vacation rentals) and the appeals section 2.50.080. This is the current watch item on the Palm Springs ordinance page.

November 12, 2025

Ordinance No. 2118 — annual contract caps confirmed

Removed a scheduled reduction that would have taken effect January 1, 2026. Annual rental-contract limits: 26 contracts per calendar year for permittees who applied after October 17, 2022; 32 contracts for those on or before that date (plus up to four more during the third quarter).

Registration fees increased (Resolution No. 25344)

New/annual vacation rental registration: $1,046. Junior vacation rental: $523. Homeshare: $261. Adopted September 25, 2025.
