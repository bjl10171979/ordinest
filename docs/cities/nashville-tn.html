Short-term rental alerts — Nashville, TN

In Nashville and Davidson County you must hold a Short Term Rental Property (STRP) permit from Metro Codes before you list. Permit types, renewal timing, and operating rules all live on Metro's official pages — Ordinest checks them daily.

Recent official activity

March 11, 2026

New online permit application process

Metro launched a new online STRP application process. Important rules: submit once — do not file a second application — and handle correspondence through strpapplications@nashville.gov. Documents must be ready to upload; you cannot save and return.

Effective April 7, 2026

Zoning letter fee introduced

Metro began assessing a fee for zoning letters — relevant if your permit path requires a zoning determination.

Official source →
Standing rule — renewals

Permits are valid 365 days

STRP permits must be renewed annually before expiration. System renewal emails go out at 60 and 45 days before expiry — Metro explicitly warns to check spam folders.
